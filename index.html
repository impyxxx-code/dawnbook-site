A private diary of your child's life, to hand to them one day.

Voice notes, photos, words, milestones and letters, kept safe and encrypted so only your family can read them. Add a memory in seconds. Turn each year into a storybook to share.

Private by design

Your memories live on your phone. Optional backup is encrypted with a key only you hold. There are no ads, no tracking and no selling of data.

Family can add their own

Invite grandparents and friends to send a photo, a few words or a voice note. They never see the diary, and nothing is added until you approve it.

A storybook every year

Make a designed PDF of a year of memories to share with family. Letters you keep sealed are never included.
